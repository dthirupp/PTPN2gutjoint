The R scripts here were used to analyze the microbiome datasets generated in the study "Interaction between haploinsufficiency of PTPN2 and patient microbiome promotes autoimmune arthritis in mice". doi: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaut.2025.103452

# Highlights of the study
* SKG mice were used to model interactions between gut microbiota and the rheumatoid arthritis (RA) PTPN2 gene.

* Microbiota from RA patients induced variable arthritis in PTPN2+/+ and PTPN2+/− germ-free SKG mice.

* Two RA microbiota RA#86 and RA#02 induced more arthritis in PTPN2+/− germ-free SKG mice. The RA#86 microbiome was found to carry a significant expansion of Prevotella copri, an RA-associated pathobiont.

* Monocolonization with P. copri caused enhanced arthritis in PTPN2+/− germ-free SKG mice similar to RA#86 microbiome.

# Abstract of the study:

Gut dysbiosis is observed in patients with rheumatoid arthritis (RA) and juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA), however, how it promotes disease in interaction with other environmental and genetic risk factors remains unclear. Here we assessed interactions between gut dysbiosis and RA/JIA-associated loss of function haplotypes of the RA/JIA-associated PTPN2 gene by inducing mannan-induced arthritis in germ-free PTPN2+/+ and PTPN2 haploinsufficient (PTPN2+/−) SKG mice reconstituted with fecal microbiota from six patients with seropositive RA. Mannan-induced arthritis and lymph node T cell immunophenotypes were identical in germ free PTPN2+/+ vs PTPN2+/− SKG mice. While no difference in arthritis severity was seen among PTPN2+/+ mice recipient of RA gut microbiota, two microbiomes (RA#02 and RA#86) enhanced arthritis in PTPN2+/− mice. The microbiome of RA patient microbiota recipient mice exclusively clustered by patient of origin and the RA#86 microbiome was found to carry a significant expansion of Prevotella genera, which is associated with RA dysbiosis. RA#86 microbiota-recipient PTPN2+/− mice selectively displayed increased joint GM-CSF expression and an expansion of CD4+RORγt+FoxP3− T cells in the joints, without evidence of increased intestinal inflammation, gut barrier leakage or expansion of P. copri in post-mannan fecal samples. Monocolonization with P. copri caused enhanced arthritis and CD4+RORγt+FoxP3− T cells expansion in PTPN2+/− vs PTPN2+/+ mice. Our data support current views about P. copri promotion of autoimmune arthritis and suggest that its pathogenicity can be amplified via interaction with a dysbiotic context and risk factors that enhance gut mucosa immune responses.

# Sequencing Data generation and Analyes:

Gut bacterial DNA from patient fecal samples as well as SKG-mice fecal reconstitution study samples were extracted and amplified for 16S rRNA sequencing following the Earth Microbiome Project (EMP) high throughput (HTP) DNA extraction [59] and EMP 16S Illumina Amplicon Protocol V.2 [dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.kqdg3dzzl25z/v2]. V4-V5 regions were targeted for amplification and sequencing using GTGYCAGCMGCCGCGGTAA (forward) and GGACTACNVGGGTWTCTAAT (reverse) primers respectively. Prepared libraries were sequenced using ILLUMINA MiSeq (100,000 reads per sample). Obtained sequences were imported using QIIME2 v2023.5 as EMPPairedEndSequences and demultiplexed. Paired-end reads were combined using “qiime vsearch merge-pairs” and were quality-filtered (against a positive database of 16S amplicons and a negative database of contaminants) and denoised (using the default parameters) with Deblur. Reads were trimmed as appropriate for quality (∼151 bp). β-diversity values were calculated on samples normalized by rarefaction using qiime diversity core-metrics-phylogenetic for unweighted unifrac distance matrix and jaccard distance matrix or using qiime gemelli phylogenetic-rpca for phylo-RPCA matrix. Distance matrices and ordinations generated were imported R version 3.6.3 (R Core Team, 2020) and plotted with ggplot2 (Wickham 2016, https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=ggplot2). Bacteria taxonomy was assigned to the filtered sequences with the “qiime feature-classifier classify-sklearn” command, using a scikit-learn classifier trained on Greengenes2 99 % OTU [60] and plotted in R using ggplot2. Differential abundance of significant operational taxonomic units (OTUs) was performed using ANCOM-BC [32] plugin via QIIME2 and collapsed to genus and family level using Greengenes2 99 % OTU taxonomy.
